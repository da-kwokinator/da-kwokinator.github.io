How CourseSync works

Start with your school community, build an accurate schedule, then use catalog tools, graduation tracking, and social features so every choice has context from classmates—not guesswork.

  1. 1

    Join your school & publish your profile

    Sign up for Emerald or Fallon, save your profile to the verified directory, and set privacy for what classmates can see on schedules, activities, and future plans.

  2. 2

    Plan classes, credits & what is next

    Build your current schedule with bell times and teachers, track grad requirements (high school), search the course catalog, and save primary and backup future lists by grade.

  3. 3

    Learn from your community

    Browse Trending, read peer ratings and comments, find classmates in the same period, post on the networking board, message friends, and send course recommendations.

Main features

The dashboard brings planning and people together—each panel maps to a real part of your school year.

Current schedule

Monday–Friday rows with periods, rooms, teachers, ratings, comments, and reactions tied to your community bell schedule.

Grad requirements

High school students see DUSD credit progress and UC/CSU A-G categories as courses are added—so you know what you still need.

Future courses & catalog

Primary and backup lists by grade, course browser with filters, and enrollment signals from published classmate schedules.

Community directory

Find verified students at your school, filter by grade or pathway, and open full profiles with mutual courses.

Networking board

Public or friends-only posts for coursework, study groups, and questions—with replies and reactions on each thread.

Friends & messages

Send requests, accept classmates, message friends, and see community activity from people in your school.

Classes & matching

See who shares your period, teacher, and subject—and compare schedules without digging through group chats.

Trending & course info

Discover hot courses, people, and board threads with clear reasons—recent comments, enrollment, and future-plan demand.

Benefits

CourseSync is built for decisions you actually make during the year—not just a static course list.

Know what is popular

Trending and Course Info highlight enrollment, ratings, comments, future demand, and community activity with real examples.

Stay in your community

Each account belongs to one school community, so directory members and course options stay relevant to where you actually attend.

Plan with support

Friends can react, comment, recommend courses, discuss classes, and help you compare what fits your goals.

See the full picture

Grad requirements, future lists, and your current schedule live in one place so you do not miss credits or prerequisites.

Goals

CourseSync exists so students can plan with confidence, transparency, and help from people at the same school.

Make planning visible

Turn scattered conversations into shared context: who is taking what, how hard a class feels, and which teachers or periods classmates chose.

Protect student choice

You control privacy on schedules, bio, and future plans. The directory shows verified profiles without forcing everything public.

Support the whole path

From middle school exploration to high school graduation tracking, the same workspace grows with your grade level and community.

Reduce last-minute surprises

Notifications, trending signals, and enrollment views help you notice popular courses, deadlines, and peer advice before registration windows close.
