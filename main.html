    My Snapshot

    My Snapshot is your read-only planning summary—what classmates see when they open your profile, and what you see before sharing or comparing schedules. It pulls together your active bell-schedule classes, grade-by-grade future picks (with notes when privacy allows), and summer or online courses taken outside the regular day. Use it to sanity-check your published plan, confirm which semester or day view you are showing, and mark completion dates for outside-school work once a course is finished.

    Explorer

    Browse without committing to anything. Explorer helps you test possibilities: filter course ideas by graduation requirement or level (AP, Honors, ROP, SAI, CP), discover people whose profiles can give real context, and rotate through planning facts that make your next schedule decision easier.
